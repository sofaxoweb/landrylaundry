FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Simply schedule a pickup time online or by texting us. Leave your laundry bag at your door, and we'll pick it up, clean everything, and deliver it back — usually within 24 hours. It's completely contactless and hassle-free.

We use premium, eco-friendly detergents that are tough on stains but gentle on fabrics. If you have allergies or preferences, just let us know — we're happy to use hypoallergenic or fragrance-free products.

We have a 10 lb minimum per order to ensure efficiency. Most customers easily exceed this with a standard load of laundry.

We're fully insured and bonded. In the rare event something is damaged or lost, we'll reimburse you for the full value. Your satisfaction is our top priority.

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Venmo. Payment is processed after your laundry is weighed and cleaned — no upfront charges.
